# Product

Glitch is a voice-first health coach for women in perimenopause and menopause. It
turns WHOOP wearable data (hot flashes, night sweats, sleep, HRV, blood pressure)
into simple visuals, alerts, and a doctor-ready summary.

Built for Hackachusetts 2026 (Healthcare track H1: wearable data into better care).

## Guiding principles

- Wellness insights, not a diagnosis. Treatment decisions belong with a clinician.
  Never phrase output as medical advice or a diagnosis.
- Privacy first. Personal data is encrypted on-device (AES-GCM). There is no backend
  database. Camera and mic are analysed in the browser; only text and a mood label
  leave the device.
- Ethnicity and hormone details are optional, consented, and used only for context,
  never for scoring.
- Glanceable UX: the home screen must stay simple and readable at a glance.
